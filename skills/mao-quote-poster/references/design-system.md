# Poster Design System

## Visual direction

A solemn, vintage Chinese commemorative-poster aesthetic, inspired by the supplied reference image without copying its branding or event-specific footer.

### Palette

- Main field: deep crimson / vermilion red.
- Shadow field: dark maroon and red-black.
- Highlight: warm ivory, pale gold, or muted yellow.
- Glow: soft amber, never neon orange.

### Background

- Vertical drapery or flag-like folds, primarily at the sides.
- A brighter vertical beam or central light field.
- Fine film grain and tactile print texture.
- A restrained dark-red silhouette in the lower third.
- Keep the central quote area calm and readable.

### Typography

- Main quote: bold Chinese display type, centered, high contrast.
- Attribution: smaller calligraphic or serif-like Chinese type, lower-right or centered beneath the quote.
- Preserve the supplied text exactly.
- Use balanced line lengths and comfortable spacing.
- No decorative Latin text or invented microcopy.

## Layout proportions for a 9:16 canvas

- Top breathing room: approximately 14–19% of canvas height.
- Main quotation: approximately 24–58% of canvas height, adjusted for length.
- Attribution: directly below the quotation with clear separation.
- Silhouette: lower third, subtle and low contrast.
- Bottom breathing room: approximately 8–12%.

## Do not include by default

- Anniversary dates or memorial statements.
- Publisher or campaign logos.
- Flags, emblems, seals, badges, QR codes, or watermarks.
- Additional quotations or explanatory copy.
- Any wording not supplied or explicitly requested by the user.
